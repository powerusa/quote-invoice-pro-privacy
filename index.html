Privacy Policy

Quote & Invoice Pro
Developer: Powerusa
Last updated: September 29, 2026

Quote & Invoice Pro helps you create quotes, invoices, and PDF documents. The app does not require an account. We do not collect or receive the business, client, or document information you enter in the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores information locally on your device, including business details, client names and contact details, tax identifiers, addresses, service items, quotes, invoices, payment records, notes, and settings. This information is used to provide the app’s features and generate your documents. It is not sent to a developer-operated server.

PDF exports and sharing

PDF documents are generated on your device. When you choose to share or save a PDF, the document is provided to the destination or service you select using the iOS share sheet. Recipients and services handle that document according to their own practices. Exported copies remain outside the app’s control.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Apple services and backups

Apple handles App Store downloads and any purchases through its services. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process platform diagnostics and purchase information under its own privacy policy and your device settings. Local app data may be included in backups managed by iOS, iCloud, or your computer, depending on your settings. The app does not provide its own cloud synchronization service.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device while the app and its data are retained. Deleting the app through iOS removes its local app data; offloading the app can retain data. Backups and PDFs you have saved or shared must be managed separately in the relevant service or location. We cannot access, retrieve, or delete your local records remotely.

Your choices and security

You choose what information to enter and whether to export or share it. Protect your device with a passcode and keep iOS up to date. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in your documents. No storage or sharing method can guarantee absolute security.

Children

The app is intended for business use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Powerusa through the privacy-policy support page. GitHub issues are public: do not include client details, invoice contents, or other confidential information. GitHub handles information submitted to its website under its own privacy policy.

This policy applies to the Quote & Invoice Pro iOS app. This policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information when you visit it.
